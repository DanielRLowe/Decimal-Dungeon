Historical country figures and flags

The three maps show July 1, 1886, July 1, 1914, and August 31, 1939. A profile uses statistical records for that particular year. The figures have different reporting dates and geographic coverage.

Population and military personnel

Population
Most figures come from the Correlates of War Project’s National Material Capabilities version 7.0. In a country-year where that source has no figure, a matched Maddison economic series can also supply a midyear population estimate. The profile names the source used. Many early figures are estimates or interpolations between censuses. The source reports thousands of people. For instance, its 1914 United States observation of 99,111 represents about 99,111,000 people.
Military personnel
The project measures regular national forces at the start of the year. Its count includes the army, navy and air forces where applicable. Reserves, temporary mobilizations later in the year and separately administered colonial units generally fall outside this definition. The project reports thousands. A recorded zero can mean a force too small to count at this precision, or forces outside the source’s definition.
Geographic scope
A country shown on the political map can include territories outside the statistical series. In particular, the British Empire’s population figure uses the United Kingdom series. The country profile explains special cases. For the early Serbian state, the COW observations use its combined Serbia/Yugoslavia series. For Austria-Hungary, the game uses the original combined state record. In the 1886 Swedish–Norwegian union, the two countries shared a monarch. Norwegian Parliament on the 1814–1905 union.
Missing values
Some states on the CShapes map lack observations in the COW series. Missing entries are omitted unless the same-year Maddison population is available for the matched economy. An available population can still be shown when the military observation is missing.

Economic output

GDP estimates come from the Maddison Project Database 2023. The game multiplies the project’s real GDP per person by its own midyear population estimate for the same place and year. The result is rounded to the nearest million 2011 international dollars. These units adjust for differences in price levels. They describe estimated purchasing power and cannot be directly compared with modern-profile GDP in current US dollars.

The named economy can cover different territory from the political map. The United Kingdom series, for instance, describes the domestic economy. Single successor-country estimates are omitted for composite empires such as Austria-Hungary and the Ottoman Empire. An absent same-year observation remains unavailable.

Period flags

Each historical flag’s profile links to the source for that design. Illustrations use simple shapes, or an emoji when its design agrees with the period flag. The 1886 United States flag uses a five-row arrangement seen on a surviving artifact. The 1914 and 1939 flags use the standardized six rows of eight stars.
